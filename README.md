# GrowthOS

GrowthOS is the autonomous growth operating system for service businesses.

It is designed to continuously understand a business, identify the highest-value growth opportunities, execute approved actions across marketing and customer-conversion channels, measure real business outcomes, and learn what to do next.

GrowthOS is **strategically part of Wiserr** but **technically isolated as its own service/repository** while the operating model is being proven.

> Status: architecture + control-plane foundation. Not yet a production autonomous marketing platform.

See `docs/VISION.md`, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, and `docs/ROADMAP.md`.
